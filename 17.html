What’s in City Prop A

Two training sites

Phase 1 · already under way

LETC at UNT Dallas

  • About 20 acres at I-35 and I-20
  • $227M total cost; $50M from 2024 bond
  • Broke ground Sept. 3
  • City Prop A share: $82M

Phase 2 · can still be stopped

Training & tactical complex at Dallas Executive Airport

  • Split off in ‘25 because UNT Dallas was too small and the university doesn’t want a shooting range on campus.
  • Driving course (EVOC) and village simulation
  • City Prop A share: $149M
  • Needs this bond to move forward

Sources: City LETC memo, Apr. 13, 2026; council briefings, Mar. 5, 2025 and Aug. 5, 2026; NBC 5 DFW, Sept. 3, 2026
