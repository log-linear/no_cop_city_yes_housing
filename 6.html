Know your ballot

Two Prop As, two different votes

City of Dallas Prop A: vote NO

  • Borrows $443M; taxpayers repay about $630M
  • 86% goes to police buildings, and the ballot names no projects
  • Sent to voters by City Council, 11–4

Dallas County Prop A: vote YES

  • Adds 2.4¢ per $100 to the county tax rate: about $24 a year per $100K of taxable value
  • Raises about $103M a year for homelessness, mental health care and stable housing
  • Sent to voters by Commissioners Court, 3–2

Only Dallas County voters see the County Prop A. Read the heading above each one.

Sources: City bond ordinance; Dallas County Election Order and tax rate fact sheet; Dallas Free Press, Oct. 2, 2026
